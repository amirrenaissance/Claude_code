## Send settings
- Daily cap: 30–40 per inbox. 2,500 contacts over 3 steps ≈ 7,500 sends, so you need about 10 warmed inboxes for a 3-week run.
- Plain text, one link, no tracking pixel on email 1 (better Gmail/Yahoo inbox placement).
- Verify the list first and drop invalid or catch-all addresses before upload.
- Remove anyone who replies "no thanks" from every campaign and blocklist them.

## Funnel math (assumptions, adjust after week 1)
| Stage | Rate | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered | 90% | ~2,250 |
| Applied | 4–6% | 90–135 |
| Pass screen + roleplay | 30% | 27–40 |
| Hired | 50% of passers | 13–20 setters |

That's about 160k credits for ~15 hires, roughly 10k credits per hire. Compare that with job-board cost per hire before scaling this channel.

## Compliance flags
- **PH Data Privacy Act (RA 10173):** these emails were sourced without consent. Make the first email say who you are and why you're writing, honour every opt-out, and delete non-responders after this hiring push.
- **Contractor classification:** say "independent contractor" in the job post and offer letter, not "employee". Avoid wording about fixed schedules or company control beyond what's needed for shift coverage.
- **Non-solicitation:** don't target your own clients' setters. Exclude any Renaissance partner companies from the list before sending.
